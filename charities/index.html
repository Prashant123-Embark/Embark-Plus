      Frequently asked questions.

      What charity teams ask before they book a demo.

      Does Embark+ replace our existing creative team?

      No. It gives your team a way to create and adapt fundraising content with less repeated production work. Your team retains control of its brand, messaging and approvals.

      Can we use our existing brand guidelines?

      Your brand requirements and approved assets are set up as part of onboarding. We'll show you the brand controls available in the demo.

      Can content be adapted for different markets?

      Yes. Embark+ can adapt content for different markets, languages, audiences and donation values. Local requirements and nuances should be part of the brief and review process.

      What types of content can it produce?

      Static posts, carousels, stories and reels, in multiple languages. Reels can carry voiceovers in multiple languages too.

      How much does it cost?

      Pricing has three parts: a one-time setup fee of USD 2,000, USD 1.50 per creative produced, and USD 1,500 a month for platform and service management. In the demo we'll show you what that means for your own volumes.

      How do we get started?

      Book a demonstration using one of your existing fundraising appeals. The team will show you the workflow and discuss what would be required to get started.
