      Frequently asked questions

      The questions businesses ask before they book a demo.

      Why do I need Embark+ if I already use ChatGPT or Claude?

      ChatGPT and Claude are general-purpose AI assistants. Embark+ is a branded content creation engine built around a focused content production workflow.

      It brings brand setup, the use of your own brand assets, content creation, in-platform editing, review and approval into one experience.

      How is Embark+ different from Canva?

      Canva is a design tool that gives users the ability to create their own designs.

      Embark+ focuses on branded content production using your own assets, with brand setup, creation, editing, review and approval as part of the workflow.

      I already work with an agency or a designer. Is Embark+ for me?

      Embark+ is built to make recurring content production more accessible and affordable. It is not designed to replace every agency or specialist creative service.

      Will the content look like my brand?

      Brand setup is handled during onboarding. Your own brand assets, products, shoots and media are used as the basis for content creation.

      Can I use my own images and videos?

      Yes. You can upload your own brand assets and use supported connected sources.

      Can I edit the content before approving it?

      Yes. Content can be reviewed and edited directly inside the platform before approval and delivery.

      What types of content can I create?

      Static posts, stories, carousels and reels, with headlines and designs in different formats, platforms and languages. You can see examples in the creative gallery.

      Is Embark+ a complete marketing platform today?

      Embark+ is being developed as an integrated marketing platform covering seven capabilities.

      Currently, Content Creation is the only supported capability. The other six capabilities are planned for future releases.

      How does the pricing work?

      Embark+ has three commercial components:

      • AED 7,500 one-time setup.
      • AED 6 per creative.
      • AED 1,850 monthly management.

      Platform usage is charged according to the number of creatives produced, in addition to monthly management.

      What does the monthly management fee include?

      The monthly management fee is AED 1,850 and covers platform support and account management.

      What does the one-time setup fee cover?

      The one-time setup fee is AED 7,500 and is mandatory. The setup process is best discussed during your demo, where we can walk you through it for your business.
